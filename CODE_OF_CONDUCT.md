# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to maintaining a welcoming, professional environment for everyone contributing to or using Onlooker Community projects, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

---

## Standards

**Expected behavior:**
- Be direct and constructive in technical disagreements
- Assume good faith in ambiguous situations
- Keep discussions focused on the work, not the person
- Accept maintainer decisions on scope and direction, even when you disagree
- Follow the contribution process in CONTRIBUTING.md

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Personal attacks, insults, or harassment of any kind
- Publishing private information about others without consent
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews
- Deliberate misrepresentation of others' positions

---

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to **conduct@onlooker.dev**.

Responses to violations:
1. **Warning** — for first or minor violations
2. **Temporary ban** — for repeated or more serious violations
3. **Permanent ban** — for severe violations or failure to change after repeated warnings

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any official communication channels associated with `onlooker-community` repositories.
